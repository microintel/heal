About these tones

Heal generates pure tones for relaxation, focus and sound exploration. Frequencies such as 528 Hz or 432 Hz are popular in sound-healing culture, but they are not a substitute for medical care and have no clinically proven therapeutic effect. Keep volume low, especially with headphones, and stop if you feel discomfort. The Irritating preset is intentionally harsh — use it briefly.
